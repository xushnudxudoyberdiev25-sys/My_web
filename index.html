About Me

// my journey into technology & cybersecurity

I was born in Sirdarya on December 12, 2008. I studied at School No. 35 and graduated in 2026.


Since childhood, I have been genuinely interested in technology. This curiosity motivated me to start learning programming, which led me to join Najot Ta'lim. I studied there for four months, followed by one month focused on cybersecurity.


I eventually decided to continue my cybersecurity journey independently. I discovered free educational resources and started exploring cybersecurity through online courses, videos, and practical learning. This experience taught me that learning does not always have to happen inside a classroom — curiosity and consistency can take you a long way.


After graduating from school, I carefully considered my university options. While following the latest developments in technology and education, I discovered Cyber University. The university caught my attention because of its focus on technology and cybersecurity, as well as its connection with the government.


Choosing Cyber University became an important step in my journey into technology and cybersecurity. Today, I am continuing to develop my knowledge in cybersecurity, Linux, networking, programming, and information technologies.
